# G.L.A.S.S: Remastered

[![](https://cdn.imb11.dev/mineblock%20badge_64h.png)](https://discord.gg/UzHtJKqHny)

**Turn a wall of glass into a projection of your world.**

## Usage

First, you'll need to craft a **G.L.A.S.S Terminal** and place it in your world where you want the projection capture to be. Once you've placed it, right click to open the GUI and create and select a channel, or just use the Default channel that's premade for your first terminal.

![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/5TABPEIf/images/86766741dff03535ddeb90f1ba695b025283b412.png)

![Terminal GUI](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/5TABPEIf/images/04f3c25bfc4135d136d3c09bbc5af65d21210430.png)

Once you've placed and connected your terminal to a channel, you can start making your projection! You'll need to craft **Powerable Glass**, which can be made by smelting **Redstone Infused Sand** in a furnace, which you can make by combining one **Redstone dust** and one **Sand** block.

You will need to craft a few **G.L.A.S.S Projectors** - one projector can cover up to 400 **Powerable Glass** panels. A panel is just a side of the **Powerable Glass** block - its not nessecarily equal to one block if you let the projection cover multiple sides of the powerable glass!

![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/5TABPEIf/images/7579ef85ce31db77fb927dfdab9ce38f50924e43.png)

Once you've got all the materials needed, you can start building! Place your **Powerable Glass** down and place your **G.L.A.S.S Projector**. The glowing side of the **G.L.A.S.S Projector** must be flush with the powerable glass in the direction you are projecting to. Once done, right click to open the GUI, select a channel, and give it some redstone power!


![gallery-image](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/5TABPEIf/images/3ee7b261e1c9948160e91fb5b23ce0c34c71ef28.png)

## Features
- You can crossfade multiple channels if the two projectors share the same Powerable Glass. Try it out!
- Anti-Xray support: The projection will become noisy if you position yourself in a way which noclips your view into blocks/the terrain.
- **Iris and Sodium** are supported.
